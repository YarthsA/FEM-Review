## Alternative numerical methods

• Other numerical solution methods:

\- Finite differences

» Approximates the derivatives in the differential equation using difference equations.  
» Useful for solving heat transfer and fluid mechanics problems.  
» Works well for two-dimensional regions with boundaries parallel to the coordinate axes.  
» Cumbersome when regions have curved boundaries.

• Weighted residual methods (not confined to a small subdomain):

» Collocation  
» Subdomain  
» Least squares\*  
» Galerkin's method\*

• Variational Methods\* (not confined to a small subdomain)

## Advantages of FEA

Versatile  
No geometric restriction  
Boundary conditions and loading are not restricted  
Material properties are not restricted  
Components that have different behaviours  
An FE structures can closely resembles the actual body  
The approximation is easily improved by grading the mesh or increasing the order of elements  
Can deal with multi-physics problems.

## Element strain field

$$
\varepsilon (x) = \frac {d u}{d x} = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} d N _ {i} & d N _ {j} \\ \hline d x & d x \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{c} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\}
$$

$$
{\frac {d x}{d \xi}} = {\frac {d N _ {i}}{d \xi}} x _ {i} + {\frac {d N _ {j}}{d \xi}} x _ {j} = - {\frac {x _ {i}}{2}} + {\frac {x _ {j}}{2}} = {\frac {x _ {j} - x _ {i}}{2}} = {\frac {l}{2}}
$$

$$
{\frac {d N _ {i}}{d x}} = {\frac {d N _ {i}}{d \xi}} {\frac {d \xi}{d x}} = \left(- {\frac {1}{2}}\right) \left({\frac {2}{l}}\right)
$$

$$
\frac {d N _ {j}}{d x} = \frac {d N _ {j}}{d \xi} \frac {d \xi}{d x} = \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) \left(\frac {2}{l}\right)
$$

$$
\varepsilon = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} - \frac {1}{l} & \frac {1}{l} \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{c} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\}
$$

## Element stress field

$$
\sigma (x) = E \varepsilon (x) = E \left[ \frac {d N _ {i}}{d x} - \frac {d N _ {j}}{d x} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{l} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\}
$$

$$
\sigma = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} - \frac {E}{l} & \frac {E}{l} \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{c} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\}
$$

Note: element strain and element stress are constant

## Element internal force

$$
N (x) = \sigma (x) A = E A \left[ \frac {d N _ {i}}{d x} - \frac {d N _ {j}}{d x} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{l} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\}
$$

$$
N = \left[ - \frac {E A}{l} \quad \frac {E A}{l} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{l} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\}
$$

## Element strain energy

$$
\begin{array}{l} U = \frac {1}{2} \int_ {0} ^ {V} \sigma \varepsilon d V = \frac {A l}{2} \left[ u _ {i} - u _ {j} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{c} - \frac {E}{l} \\ \frac {E}{l} \end{array} \right\} \left[ \begin{array}{c c} - \frac {1}{l} & \frac {1}{l} \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{c} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\} \\ = \frac {1}{2} \left[ \begin{array}{l l} u _ {i} & u _ {j} \end{array} \right] \left[ \begin{array}{c c} \frac {E A}{l} & - \frac {E A}{l} \\ - \frac {E A}{l} & \frac {E A}{l} \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{l} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\} \\ \end{array}

## Principle of minimum potential energy for a single element

$$
\Pi = U + W = \frac {1}{2} \left[ \begin{array}{l l} u _ {i} & u _ {j} \end{array} \right] \left[ \begin{array}{c c} \frac {E A}{l} & - \frac {E A}{l} \\ - \frac {E A}{l} & \frac {E A}{l} \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{l} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\} - \left[ \begin{array}{l l} u _ {i} & u _ {j} \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{c} \frac {q l}{2} \\ q \frac {l}{2} \end{array} \right\} - \left[ \begin{array}{l l} u _ {i} & u _ {j} \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{l} P _ {i} \\ P _ {j} \end{array} \right\}
$$

$$
\frac {\partial \Pi}{\partial \mathbf {u} ^ {T}} = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} \frac {E A}{l} & - \frac {E A}{l} \\ - \frac {E A}{l} & \frac {E A}{l} \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{l} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\} - \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {q l}{2} \\ \frac {q l}{2} \end{array} \right\} - \left\{ \begin{array}{l} P _ {i} \\ P _ {j} \end{array} \right\} = 0
$$

$$
\left[ \begin{array}{c c} \frac {E A}{l} & - \frac {E A}{l} \\ - \frac {E A}{l} & \frac {E A}{l} \end{array} \right] \left\{ \begin{array}{c} u _ {i} \\ u _ {j} \end{array} \right\} = \left\{ \begin{array}{c} \frac {q l}{2} + P _ {i} \\ q \frac {l}{2} + P _ {j} \end{array} \right\}
$$

Element equilibrium equation

$$
\mathbf {k u} = \mathbf {f}
$$

where

$$
\mathbf {k} = \int_ {V} \left(\mathbf {B} ^ {\mathrm{T}} E \mathbf {B}\right) d V
$$

is the element stiffness matrix.

## Global dynamic equations

$$
[ \mathbf {M} ] \{\ddot {\mathbf {D}} \} + [ \mathbf {C} ] \{\dot {\mathbf {D}} \} + [ \mathbf {K} ] \{\mathbf {D} \} = \left\{\mathbf {R} ^ {\mathrm{ext}} \right\}
$$

## Explicit integration

$$
\frac {1}{\Delta t ^ {2}} \left[ \mathbf {M} \right] \left\{\mathbf {D} \right\} _ {n + 1} = \left\{\mathbf {R} ^ {\mathrm{ext}} \right\} _ {n} - \left[ \mathbf {K} - \frac {2}{\Delta t ^ {2}} \mathbf {M} + \frac {1}{\Delta t} \mathbf {C} \right] \left\{\mathbf {D} \right\} _ {n} - \left[ \frac {2}{\Delta t ^ {2}} \mathbf {M} - \frac {1}{\Delta t} \mathbf {C} \right] \left\{\mathbf {D} \right\} _ {n - 1}
$$

## Implicit integration

$$
\left[ \mathbf {K} ^ {\mathrm{eff}} \right] \left\{\mathbf {D} \right\} _ {n + 1} = \left\{\mathbf {R} ^ {\mathrm{ext}} \right\} _ {n + 1} + \left[ \mathbf {M} \right] \left[ \frac {1}{\beta \Delta t ^ {2}} \left\{\mathbf {D} \right\} _ {n} + \frac {1}{\beta \Delta t} \left\{\dot {\mathbf {D}} \right\} _ {n} + \left(\frac {1}{2 \beta} - 1\right) \left\{\ddot {\mathbf {D}} \right\} _ {n} \right]
$$

$$
+ \left[ \mathbf {C} \right] \left[ \frac {\gamma}{\beta \Delta t} \left\{\mathbf {D} \right\} _ {n} + \left(\frac {\gamma}{\beta} - 1\right) \left\{\dot {\mathbf {D}} \right\} _ {n} + \Delta t \left(\frac {\gamma}{2 \beta} - 1\right) \left\{\ddot {\mathbf {D}} \right\} _ {n} \right]
$$

where

$$
\left[ \mathbf {K} ^ {\mathrm{eff}} \right] = \frac {1}{\beta \Delta t ^ {2}} \left[ \mathbf {M} \right] + \frac {\gamma}{\beta \Delta t} \left[ \mathbf {C} \right] + \left[ \mathbf {K} \right]
$$
